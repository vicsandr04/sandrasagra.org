Navigation

Find a relation

Select the input field, enter all or part of a name, then press Return. Selecting the field clears its current text without changing the person in focus. To trace a relationship directly, enter two names separated by a forward slash, such as “First Person / Second Person,” then press Return.

Explore the family

Drag empty space to move through the chart. Use the mouse wheel or pinch gesture to zoom. Click a person on desktop, or double-tap on touch, to change focus.

View tooltips

Hover over a person with a mouse. On a touch screen, tap once to show their full name, nicknames, and relationship to the focused person.

Trace a relationship

Right-click a person, or press and hold on a touch screen, then choose the relationship-path option. The people and connections between that person and the focused individual will be highlighted.
